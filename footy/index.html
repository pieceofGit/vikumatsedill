Who has the rights

The British rights are carved up by competition and by kickoff slot. Once you know the slot, you know the channel.

Why some Saturday matches are on no channel at all

UK broadcasters are not allowed to show any football live between roughly 14:45 and 17:15 on a Saturday. It is a long-standing rule meant to protect attendances in the lower leagues, and it is why a 15:00 Saturday kickoff in the Premier League appears here as not shown live. Those matches turn up as highlights on Match of the Day on BBC One later the same night, and on BBC iPlayer afterwards.

Why later matches say “not selected yet”

Broadcasters choose which matches they want roughly five to six weeks in advance, and the kickoff times move when they do. Anything beyond that window is shown here on its provisional Saturday-afternoon slot, with no channel attached, because no channel has picked it yet.
